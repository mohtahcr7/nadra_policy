Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nadra. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when using our app. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Nadra is designed to work completely offline. We do not collect, store, or send any personal data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Geolocation data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage or behavioral data
  • Any data leaving your device

3. Data Storage – Local Only

All data entered in the app (plant names, watering schedules, notes, photos) is stored exclusively on your device using local storage.

  • Your data never leaves your device
  • No one can access it except you
  • Data is permanently deleted upon uninstall
  • You can manually delete it from settings

4. Photos and Media

The app may request access to your photo gallery to add plant images. This permission is only used to display images within the app. Photos are stored locally and are not uploaded or sent anywhere.

5. Notifications

The app uses local notifications (scheduled on your device) to remind you of watering times. These notifications:

  • Are fully created and scheduled on your device
  • Do not involve any external server or internet connection
  • Can be disabled anytime from device settings

6. Internet Access

Nadra does not require an internet connection and does not send any network requests. The app works fully offline.

7. Third-Party Services

Nadra does not include any analytics, ads, or tracking services. The app does not contain:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase
  • Facebook SDK
  • AdMob or any ad network
  • Crashlytics or error tracking services
  • Any external SDK that collects data

8. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time via:

  • Settings → Delete All Data
  • Or by uninstalling the app

No request from us is required, as we do not retain any of your data.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review the policy periodically.
